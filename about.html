ACT I // OUR STANDARDS

The Four Pillars of Craftsmanship

Every detail from our scissor selection to our sterilization protocol is designed to exceed hospital-grade salon benchmarks.

✂️

Japanese Cobalt Blades

We craft our signature cuts using hand-honed Japanese cobalt alloy scissors. The convex razor edges slice through cuticles cleanly without splitting ends or crushing hair shafts.

🛡️

Medical Autoclave UV

No compromise on cleanliness. Metal scissors, combs, and trimmer guards undergo ultrasonic wash followed by 134°C autoclave steam and germicidal UV illumination in sealed sterilization pouches.

🌱

Conscious Formulations

We choose ammonia-free colors, paraben-free treatment masques, and dermatologically tested botanical skincare products that respect skin barrier integrity.

💆

Holistic Hospitality

From heated eye compresses during hair washes to acupressure head massages and bespoke music acoustics, your visit is a restorative break from a demanding schedule.
